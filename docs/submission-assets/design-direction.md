# Shiftcraft

Factory maintenance is expressed through precise geometry, clear connections and a quiet sense of machinery in motion. The visual reference is an equipment drawing: measured margins, restrained marks and components arranged with the care of a maintained production line.

Charcoal evokes the machine enclosure; warm orange identifies responsibility and action. Pale steel backgrounds give the system room to breathe. Color remains purposeful, allowing the architecture's service families to retain their meaning.

The original factory mark combines a sawtooth roof with a completion check. Its outline remains legible at small scale, while a separate badge suggests a repair that has passed verification. Meticulously crafted spacing keeps the mark distinct from the technical labels.

Industrial detail belongs at the edges. A small conveyor elevation, fine measurement ticks and a narrow safety stripe establish the setting without obscuring a connection. The architecture remains the primary reading experience, with painstaking attention to alignment and hierarchy.
